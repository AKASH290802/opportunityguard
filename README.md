   ## Example queries
   1)I received this WhatsApp message regarding an AI Engineer job in Bengaluru:

"Congratulations! You have been selected for the AI Engineer position. Your salary will be ₹12 LPA. To receive your official offer letter, you must pay ₹2,500 as a registration and verification fee immediately. Pay today to secure your position."

Please investigate this recruitment message.

1. Identify the suspicious indicators, especially the upfront payment request.
2. Search the web for relevant information about this recruitment practice.
3. Identify what additional information is needed to verify the company and recruiter.
4. Assess the risk based on the available evidence.
5. Recommend specific steps I should take before proceeding.

Separate verified facts from warning signs and unverified claims. Include source links for any web evidence you find. Do not invent a company name or claim to have verified the recruiter.

2)I received a message saying:

"Congratulations! Your application has been shortlisted. Our company will contact you shortly regarding the next steps."

The company name, recruiter's name, sender's email address, job description, and job link are not available.

Please assess this recruitment message.

1. Identify the information currently available.
2. Explain whether there is enough evidence to determine if the opportunity is genuine or suspicious.
3. Identify the specific information you need to investigate it properly.
4. Ask me for the most important missing details, such as the company name, recruiter's email address, job URL, or complete message.
5. Do not make an unsupported high-risk or low-risk judgment.

Explain the difference between insufficient evidence and evidence of fraud.

3)Please investigate this job opportunity as part of a recruitment verification check.

Company: Google
Job title: Cloud AI Engineer
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

Job listing: https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/132890564369490630-senior-software-engineer-ai-innovation-and-research-engagements?location=India&page=2&q=%22Artificial+Intelligence%22OR%22Machine+learning%22&src=Online%2FLinkedIn%2Flinkedin_paid_ML

Please:
1. Check whether the URL belongs to Google's official careers domain and whether the page is accessible.
2. Search Google's official careers website for the Cloud AI Engineer role.
3. Compare the job title, location, and available requirements with the listing.
4. Explain what you could verify and what remains uncertain.
5. Distinguish verification of the job listing from verification of a recruiter or individual job offer.
6. Provide the actual source links you checked.

Do not assume that the recruiter is genuine merely because a matching job exists. Do not invent evidence or claim to have verified information that you could not access.
